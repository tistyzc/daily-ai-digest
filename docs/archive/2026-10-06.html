  • AI governance has evolved through three stages: Chatbot (output safety), Copilot (decision fairness), and Agent (action safety), with the safety object shifting from Output to Action.
  • China's May 2025 'Implementation Opinions on Standardized Application and Innovative Development of Agents' proposes agent registration platforms, digital identity, capability declarations, permission management, and behavior fences.
  • Authorization in the agent era becomes a runtime object with fields like principal, task, budget, permissions, expiry, redelegation depth, and revocation — making human agency machine-readable and machine-enforceable.
💡 Why it matters: Developers building agent products must now design for identity, scoped permissions, runtime policy enforcement, and audit trails — treating each tool call as a governed action rather than a simple function invocation.
🇨🇳 做Agent产品的开发者需要把身份、权限范围、运行时策略拦截和审计日志纳入设计，把每次工具调用当作受治理的行动，而不是简单的函数调用。
