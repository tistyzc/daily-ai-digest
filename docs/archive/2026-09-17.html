当AI拥有“工龄”：下一场企业竞争，不再比拼谁的模型更聪明

虎嗅 (AI原生100等)

Drawing on a Sequoia Capital discussion with Trajectory co-founder Arjun Karanam, Dr. Xi Chunying argues that as frontier models converge in raw capability, enterprise AI competition is shifting from model intelligence to accumulated organizational experience—what he calls AI 'work tenure.' Most companies today capture none of the correction signals generated daily by AI agents, so their AI works every day yet never gains seniority; the real moat is turning data assets into governed 'experience assets.'

席春迎博士借红杉资本分享中Trajectory联创Arjun Karanam的观点指出：当各大模型通用能力越来越接近，企业比拼的不再是谁的模型更聪明，而是谁能把日常人机交互中的纠错信号沉淀下来，让AI长出“工龄”。现实很扎心——AI天天上班，却天天重新入职，经验全被浪费。真正的护城河，是把“数据资产”升级为可治理、可继承的“经验资产”。

  • Even a 'PhD-level' model is like a new hire on day one—it lacks company-specific rules, client habits, and historical judgment, not intelligence.
  • Enterprises pay for 'work tenure,' which is really accumulated judgment from thousands of real decisions and exceptions that standard processes can't cover.
  • AI agents already generate valuable learning signals daily (edits, rejections, retries), but most companies discard them, so AI never accumulates experience.
💡 Why it matters: For AI practitioners, this reframes the roadmap: the highest-leverage work is no longer model selection but building feedback capture, memory, and governance layers that turn daily human-AI interactions into durable, reusable organizational intelligence.
🇨🇳 对AI从业者来说，重点不再是选哪个模型，而是搭建反馈采集、记忆沉淀和经验治理层，把每天的人机交互变成可复用、可继承的组织智能——这才是企业AI真正的技术壁垒。

极客公园

Ant Group unveiled APASS at the 2026 Bund Summit, a trust infrastructure for agentic commerce built on the KYA (Know Your Agent) concept. It verifies agent identity through code fingerprints, runtime environments, and legal accountability, while constraining what agents may purchase via scoped, intent-aligned authorization. The system aims to resolve the dilemma of granting payment permissions to autonomous agents that operate in open, unpredictable networks.

蚂蚁集团在外滩大会发布 APASS，一套面向智能体商业的信任基础设施，核心是 KYA（认知你的智能体）。它从代码指纹、运行环境、法定责任人三个维度核验 Agent 身份，并通过意图对齐和授权边界防止「买 PPT 模板变成买黄金」。说白了，就是让开发者敢把钱包交给 AI，出了事还能找到人负责。

  • Ant Group launched APASS, a trust infrastructure for agentic commerce based on the KYA (Know Your Agent) concept, at the 2026 Bund Summit.
  • APASS verifies agent identity across three dimensions: software code fingerprints and skill manifests, runtime environment integrity via hardware-level trusted roots, and legal accountability linked to KYC/KYB systems.
  • The system uses intent-alignment models to cross-check each transaction against the user's original prompt, blocking semantic drift such as purchasing gold instead of soda water.
💡 Why it matters: This provides a concrete framework for developers building autonomous agents that handle payments, addressing the critical trust gap that currently prevents most practitioners from granting financial permissions to their agents.
🇨🇳 对开发者来说，这意味着构建能自主花钱的 Agent 终于有了可落地的信任框架，不用再因为怕出事而把支付权限死死卡住。

科技爱好者周刊（第 410 期）：你需要知道的 AI 三种机制

阮一峰周刊 🔴 Factual 🟢 Privacy

In the 410th issue of Ruan Yifeng's weekly tech digest, the author explains the three core mechanisms behind AI's ability to answer questions: the parameter mechanism (modeling human knowledge as billions of weighted token relationships), the reasoning mechanism (deriving knowledge not stored in parameters through logical inference), and the internet mechanism (using agents to search online for information beyond the model's knowledge). The issue also covers public speaking tips, tech news like a flat-pack chair and virtual avatar concerts, and articles on AI risks, model attribution, Git worktree, and web fingerprinting.

阮一峰周刊第410期用通俗易懂的方式拆解了AI回答问题的三大机制：参数机制（把人类知识压缩成数千亿个词元权重）、推理机制（靠逻辑推导出参数里没有的知识）、联网机制（通过Agent上网搜索前两者都搞不定的信息）。此外还聊了公开演讲的窍门、平板椅子、虚拟化身演唱会等科技动态，以及盖茨对AI态度的转变、GLM 5.3 Flash匿名测试等文章。
