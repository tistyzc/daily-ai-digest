诺华和BMS相继暂停了各自快速制造CAR-T疗法在自免疾病领域的临床试验，原因是出现严重安全性事件：诺华rap-cel导致3例IEC-HS死亡，BMS zola-cel出现炎症事件。快速制造通过缩短体外培养时间保留T细胞“年轻”干性，虽能降低成本并增强疗效，但也可能引发更强的体内扩增和毒性，在自免患者中风险更高。这迫使行业重新审视快速制造在自免领域的风险获益比，提醒开发者不能以牺牲安全性为代价追求速度和成本。

  • Novartis paused all 8 clinical trials of rap-cel in autoimmune and neurological diseases after 3 patient deaths from IEC-HS, a severe hyperinflammatory syndrome.
  • BMS also paused enrollment for zola-cel due to transient but reversible inflammatory events, citing precautionary reasons.
  • Rapid manufacturing platforms like T-Charge (Novartis) and NEX-T (BMS) reduce production time to ~2 days, preserving T-cell stemness but potentially increasing in vivo expansion and toxicity.
💡 Why it matters: This matters because it challenges the assumption that rapid manufacturing is a straightforward win for CAR-T scalability, forcing developers to reassess safety risks in autoimmune applications and potentially slowing adoption of faster production methods.
🇨🇳 这对开发者意味着，快速制造并非简单的降本增效捷径，在自免领域必须重新评估其安全性风险，否则可能导致临床暂停和监管障碍，影响整个CAR-T产业化路径。
