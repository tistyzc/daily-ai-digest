浙江大学博导研究“AI读心术”，这个赛道凭啥价值百亿

虎嗅 (AI原生100等)

A team led by Zhejiang University researcher Xu Xin has deployed an AI emotion-recognition system in a Weihai middle school that assesses 14 emotional indicators—including stress, anxiety, and depression—in just 30 seconds, with 90.8% accuracy and sub-0.01mm micro-motion detection. The project reflects a broader push by Chinese academia and industry into 'affective computing,' a field expected to grow into a hundred-billion-yuan market, with applications spanning mental health screening, elderly care, wearables, and AI companion toys.

浙大徐欣教授团队把AI情绪识别系统搬进了威海一所中学——学生对着摄像头静坐30秒，就能生成压力、焦虑、抑郁等14项情绪指标报告，准确率高达90.8%。从华为玄玑感知系统到刚拿到天使轮融资的创业公司，'AI读心术'正从实验室走向校园、养老院甚至毛绒玩具，一个号称百亿乃至千亿级的'情绪价值'赛道正在加速落地。

  • Xu Xin's team's emotion recognition system achieves 90.8% accuracy with 0.01mm micro-motion detection and under 200ms latency, supporting up to 50 people in parallel.
  • Zhejiang University has multiple teams working on emotion decoding, including the Emoface model for depression/bipolar disorder diagnosis (90%+ accuracy) and invasive brain-computer interface emotion decoding published in Nature Computational Science.
  • The global mental health crisis—over 1 billion people affected—creates urgent demand for scalable, objective diagnostic tools that AI emotion recognition can address.
💡 Why it matters: This signals that affective computing is transitioning from research labs to deployable products, opening opportunities for developers in computer vision, multimodal signal processing, and mental health tech—but also raising urgent questions about privacy, consent, and algorithmic bias in emotion detection.
