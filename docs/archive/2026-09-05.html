法国人把死人脑子接上机械手，三天后它学会了弹钢琴

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual 🟡 Bias

Researchers at the University of Montpellier connected a robotic hand to slices of post-mortem human brain tissue (from the motor cortex) and trained them to play piano notes through unsupervised sensory-motor learning. After three days of training, the brain slices achieved 58.5% accuracy in mimicking sequences, with some reaching 100%, and retained the memory for up to 17 days. The study, published on Research Square, demonstrates that living human brain tissue can learn new associations without consciousness or reward, offering a potential low-energy alternative to AI training.

法国蒙彼利埃大学的研究人员将死后人脑的运动皮层切片连接上机械手，通过无监督的感觉-运动关联学习，让脑片在三天内学会了弹钢琴。训练后，脑片模仿音符序列的准确率从31.2%提升到58.5%，部分脑片达到100%，且记忆能保持17天。这项研究发表在Research Square预印本上，表明离体人脑组织无需意识或奖赏就能学习，为开发超低功耗的“生物AI”提供了新思路。

  • Brain slices from deceased donors' motor cortex were cultured on microelectrode arrays and connected to a robotic hand and microphone, forming a closed-loop sensory-motor system.
  • After 900 repetitions over three days, the brain slices learned to associate specific neural activations with piano notes, improving accuracy from 31.2% (chance level) to 58.5%, with some slices achieving 100%.
  • Control experiments confirmed learning was specific to trained electrodes, was disrupted by synaptic blockers, and was impaired by neurotropic virus infection, proving genuine synaptic changes.
💡 Why it matters: This breakthrough suggests that living human brain tissue can serve as a highly energy-efficient learning substrate, potentially leading to novel biocomputing architectures that mimic biological learning with minimal power consumption.
🇨🇳 这项突破表明，活体人脑组织可作为超低功耗的学习基质，可能催生模仿生物学习的新型生物计算架构，大幅降低AI训练能耗。
