Simon Willison's Blog

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work on July 9th, a powerful but confusing product available only to paid subscribers. The article clarifies the distinction between Chat and Work, noting Work is task-oriented while Chat is for answers and brainstorming.

OpenAI 在7月9日发布了ChatGPT Work，这是一个强大但令人困惑的产品，目前仅对付费用户开放。它分为两种形态：Work Cloud（通过网页/移动端访问）和Work Local（桌面应用，前身是Codex），其中Work Cloud提供了模型选择（Sol、Luna、Terra）、代码执行环境、无头Chrome浏览器、持久化文件系统以及子代理会话等功能。文章厘清了Chat与Work的区别：Work面向有明确结果的任务，而Chat适合问答和头脑风暴。

  • ChatGPT Work is split into Work Cloud (web/mobile) and Work Local (desktop app, formerly Codex), with Work Cloud being the more interesting version.
  • Work is available only to paid subscribers ($20/month and up), not free or Go users.
  • Work Cloud offers unique features: model selection (Sol, Luna, Terra), code execution with internet, headless Chrome, persistent filesystem, ChatGPT Sites publishing, sub-agent sessions, and scheduled automations.
💡 Why it matters: This clarifies the practical differences between ChatGPT Chat and Work, helping developers choose the right tool for task automation and agentic workflows, and highlights new capabilities like sub-agents and persistent environments that could reshape how AI tasks are executed.
🇨🇳 这有助于开发者理解Chat与Work的实际差异，从而在任务自动化和智能体工作流中选择合适的工具，并凸显了子代理和持久化环境等新能力，可能重塑AI任务执行的方式。
