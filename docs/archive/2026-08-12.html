邓煜菲尔兹奖工作的物理内核：微观可逆粒子为何造就不可逆宏观世界？

虎嗅 (AI原生100等)

Yu Deng, the 2026 Fields Medalist, along with collaborators Zaher Hani and Xiao Ma, proved that the Boltzmann equation can be rigorously derived from the reversible hard-sphere dynamics of dilute gases under certain conditions, extending Lanford's theorem beyond its short-time limit. Their work provides a new rigorous foundation for understanding how macroscopic irreversibility emerges from microscopic reversible dynamics, addressing a fundamental question in statistical physics.

2026年菲尔兹奖得主邓煜与合作者Hani、马骁证明，在适当条件下，可从稀薄气体硬球的可逆动力学严格推导出玻尔兹曼方程，突破了Lanford定理只能覆盖极短时间的限制。这一成果为“大量可逆粒子如何造就不可逆宏观世界”这一基础物理问题提供了新的严格数学基础，是统计物理领域的重大突破。

  • Deng, Hani, and Ma proved that the Boltzmann equation can be rigorously derived from hard-sphere dynamics for dilute gases, extending Lanford's theorem beyond the short-time limit.
  • The proof uses a detailed classification of collision histories, including recollisions and closed loops, to control complex correlations in the low-density limit.
  • The result holds under conditions: initial states without long-range multiparticle correlations, and the existence of a controlled solution to the Boltzmann equation over the time interval.
💡 Why it matters: This work provides a rigorous mathematical bridge between microscopic reversible dynamics and macroscopic irreversible behavior, potentially influencing numerical methods and theoretical models in kinetic theory and statistical physics.
🇨🇳 该成果为微观可逆与宏观不可逆之间建立了严格的数学桥梁，可能影响动理学理论和统计物理中的数值方法与理论模型。
