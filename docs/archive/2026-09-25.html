阮一峰周刊 🔴 Factual 🟢 Privacy

In the 410th issue of Ruan Yifeng's weekly newsletter, the author explains three core mechanisms behind how large language models answer questions: the parameter mechanism (knowledge compressed into billions of weights via training), the reasoning mechanism (deriving knowledge not stored in parameters through logic), and the web-search mechanism (retrieving real-time information via agents). The issue also covers topics like public speaking tips, a foldable flat-pack chair, virtual avatar concerts, and a Git worktree tutorial.

阮一峰周刊第410期用通俗易懂的方式讲清了AI回答问题的三大机制：参数机制（把人类知识压缩进几千亿个权重里）、推理机制（靠逻辑推导出参数里没有的知识）、联网机制（通过Agent实时搜索）。此外还聊了公开演讲的窍门、可折叠平板椅、虚拟化身演唱会，以及Git worktree使用详解等有趣内容。

  • LLMs model human knowledge through a parameter mechanism: knowledge is decomposed into tokens and their relationships are encoded in billions of weights during training.
  • The reasoning mechanism allows models to derive knowledge not explicitly stored in parameters, such as calculating population growth rate from birth and death rates.
  • The web-search mechanism (via agents or application frameworks) retrieves real-time information that parameters and reasoning cannot provide, such as today's stock index.

科技爱好者周刊（第 408 期）：你需要知道的 AI 缓存知识

阮一峰周刊 🟢 Privacy

This week's tech digest explains AI prompt caching: cached input tokens can cost as little as 1/50th of uncached tokens because the model skips recomputing unchanged prefixes in multi-turn conversations. Cache lifetimes vary by provider (Anthropic 5 min, DeepSeek/OpenAI 10-30 min, Google up to 1 hour), so agents often send periodic keep-alive requests—now recommended every 4 minutes rather than 30 seconds. The issue also covers Bluetooth-reading traffic cameras, sonic fire extinguishing, windowless jets, and the longest line of sight on Earth.

本期周刊把「AI 缓存」这件事讲透了：多轮对话里那些重复的前缀，模型其实没必要每次重算，缓存命中后输入价格能低到未命中的五十分之一（比如 DeepSeek 只要 2 分钱）。但缓存有保质期——Anthropic 5 分钟、DeepSeek/OpenAI 10~30 分钟、Google 1 小时，所以 Agent 会定时「续命」，最新建议是每 4 分钟激活一次，而不是 30 秒。此外还有能读蓝牙的交通摄像头、用声波灭火、没有舷窗的飞机等趣闻。

  • Cached input tokens are dramatically cheaper (DeepSeek V4 Flash: 0.02 vs 1 yuan) because cached prefixes skip attention recomputation, costing only storage.
  • Cache TTL differs by vendor: Anthropic 5 min, DeepSeek 10 min, OpenAI 10-30 min, Google up to 1 hour; expired caches force full-price recomputation.
  • AI agents keep caches alive with periodic requests; the recommended interval has shifted from 30 seconds to 4 minutes to cut keep-alive costs.
💡 Why it matters: Developers building LLM agents should design prompt prefixes to be stable and cache-friendly, and tune keep-alive intervals—this can cut inference costs by orders of magnitude on long multi-turn workloads.
🇨🇳 做 LLM Agent 的开发者，应尽量保持提示词前缀稳定、便于命中缓存，并合理设置续命间隔，这能在长多轮任务中把推理成本降低几个数量级。
