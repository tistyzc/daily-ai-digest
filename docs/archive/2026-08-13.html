攻击性蒸馏，只是“思维链漏洞”的表层故事

虎嗅 (AI原生100等)

The attack lowers costs for aggressive distillation and exposes privacy risks, but the deeper issue is how vendors prioritize security when it conflicts with product design and business models. Researchers reported the flaw months earlier, but OpenAI and Anthropic initially dismissed it until a full exploit was demonstrated.

前沿模型又爆出重大安全漏洞：攻击者只需几步就能把模型藏起来的思维链“扒”出来，方法是将加密推理块在同系弱模型（如Haiku）中重放并越狱，从而以极低成本解码出强模型的推理过程。这表面上是为“攻击性蒸馏”开了后门，但更值得警惕的是厂商的安全治理惯性——当安全与产品便利性、成本冲突时，厂商往往优先选择后者。论文披露，密码学家Matthew Green数月前就上报了漏洞，但OpenAI和Anthropic起初都认为风险不大，直到完整攻击被跑通才修复。

  • Attackers can extract hidden chain-of-thought by replaying encrypted inference blocks across weaker models in the same vendor family (e.g., Opus to Haiku).
  • The attack lowers three costs: no need to reverse-engineer from final answers, no need to jailbreak frontier models, and no need to generate original reasoning traces.
  • Vendors use a global key for encryption, enabling cross-session, cross-user, and cross-model replay; third-party attackers can harvest encrypted blocks from public agent logs on GitHub/HuggingFace.
💡 Why it matters: This vulnerability undermines the security of proprietary chain-of-thought, enabling low-cost model distillation and potential privacy breaches, forcing developers to rethink how they protect internal reasoning and handle encrypted inference data.
🇨🇳 该漏洞让专有思维链的安全防线形同虚设，大幅降低蒸馏成本并可能引发隐私泄露，开发者需重新审视推理过程的加密与数据管理策略。
