大模型一体机市场彻底变天了：曾经动辄上百万的671B满血版卖不动了，轻量化设备成了出货主力，中端机型最多只能跑70B模型。有意思的是，内存和GPU涨价反而让去年买的一体机二手卖出还能赚钱。而DeepSeek V4.1 Flash这类新MoE模型参数更小、能力更强，老设备不加卡就能原地升级，算是给行业留了个惊喜。

  • The all-in-one LLM appliance market has bifurcated: lightweight devices (≤70B models) drive volume shipments, while full-parameter 671B machines are now niche, concentrated among SOEs and financial institutions.
  • Demand-side bubble deflation, improved model capability density (MoE, distillation, quantization), constrained high-end chip supply (H20 ban), and memory price hikes are the four main drivers of the 'shrinkage'.
  • Hardware purchased in early 2025 has appreciated in resale value due to component price increases, with some failed projects even turning a profit by reselling servers.
