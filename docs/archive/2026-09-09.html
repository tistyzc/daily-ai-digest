  • AI agents judge documentation within 500 tokens and have strict context limits, often skipping or truncating long docs.

长胶陷阱：为什么优秀的AI系统必须懂得“不优化”什么

钛媒体

This article argues that mature AI systems must know what not to optimize, using a table tennis analogy about 'long pimples' players to illustrate that absorbing rare edge cases into core systems can distort their primary function. The author proposes a 3C framework (Component, Combo, Competition) and advises setting boundaries—routing rare, costly cases to humans or rejecting them—rather than forcing fixes into core components. The key insight is that boundaries are part of the product, and optimization should align with the system's intended purpose, not just technical capability.

这篇文章通过乒乓球中“长胶”对手的比喻，指出成熟的AI系统必须懂得“不优化”什么——吸收罕见边缘案例会扭曲系统的核心功能。作者提出“3C框架”（组件、连招、实战），并建议对罕见且适配成本高的案例设立边界，路由给人工或明确拒绝，而不是强行塞进核心系统。核心观点是：边界本身就是产品的一部分，优化必须服务于系统的设计目标，而非单纯追求技术上的改进。

  • AI hallucination often stems from generating confident answers without sufficient data; the opposite is recognizing capability boundaries and stopping.
  • Rare edge cases, if absorbed into core systems, can distort the system's primary function—analogous to over-training for 'long pimple' opponents ruining a player's normal game.
  • The 3C framework (Component, Combo, Competition) guides responses: add new combos for frequent valuable cases, modify components only with strong evidence, and set boundaries for rare, costly, or distorting cases.
💡 Why it matters: This matters because it challenges the default engineering instinct to fix every failure, urging developers to consciously design boundaries that preserve core system integrity and align optimization with business goals.
🇨🇳 这对开发者很重要：它提醒我们不要默认修复所有故障，而应有意识地设计边界，保护核心系统的稳定性，并让优化服务于业务目标而非技术完美主义。
