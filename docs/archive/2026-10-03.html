This week's tech digest explains AI prompt caching: why cached input tokens cost up to 50x less than uncached ones, how long different providers keep caches (Anthropic 5 min, DeepSeek/OpenAI 10-30 min, Google up to 1 hour), and how agents keep caches warm with periodic pings. It also covers Bluetooth-reading traffic cameras, sonic fire extinguishing, windowless jets, and the longest line of sight on Earth.

本期周刊把 AI 缓存这件事讲透了：命中缓存的输入 Token 价格能便宜到未命中的五十分之一，因为省下了重复的注意力计算，只收存储费。各家缓存存活时间不同——Anthropic 只有 5 分钟，DeepSeek 和 OpenAI 约 10 分钟，Google 能撑到 1 小时，所以 Agent 们会定期发请求「续命」，但最新建议是别 30 秒一次那么勤，4 分钟一次就够了。此外还有能读蓝牙的交通摄像头、用声波灭火、没有舷窗的飞机等趣闻。

  • Cached input tokens can be up to 50x cheaper than uncached ones because cached prefixes skip recomputation and only incur storage cost.
  • Cache lifetimes vary by provider: Anthropic ~5 minutes, DeepSeek ~10 minutes, OpenAI 10-30 minutes, Google up to 1 hour.
  • AI agents keep caches alive by sending periodic requests; the recommended interval has shifted from every 30 seconds to every 4 minutes.
💡 Why it matters: Developers building LLM agents should design prompt prefixes to maximize cache hits and tune keep-alive intervals, since caching can cut inference costs by orders of magnitude.
🇨🇳 做 LLM Agent 的开发者应优化提示词前缀以提高缓存命中率，并合理设置保活间隔，缓存能把推理成本降低几个数量级。
