💡 Why it matters: Developers and AI practitioners should treat 'data deletion' and 'machine unlearning' claims with skepticism—compliance reports may assert erasure while the information persists in model weights, recoverable with minimal fine-tuning.
🇨🇳 开发者和AI从业者应对“数据删除”和“机器遗忘”的宣传保持警惕——合规报告可以声称已删除，但信息仍留在模型权重里，少量微调就能恢复。
