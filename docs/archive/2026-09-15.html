科技爱好者周刊（第 410 期）：你需要知道的 AI 三种机制

阮一峰周刊 🔴 Factual 🟢 Privacy

In the 410th issue of Ruan Yifeng's weekly newsletter, the author explains three core mechanisms behind how large language models answer questions: the parameter mechanism (knowledge compressed into billions of weights via training), the reasoning mechanism (deriving knowledge not stored in parameters through logical inference), and the web-access mechanism (using agents to search the internet for information that can't be parameterized or inferred). The issue also covers public speaking tips, tech news like a flat-pack fiberglass chair and virtual avatar concerts, and articles on AI risks, the anonymous Ox Alpha model (revealed to be Zhipu's GLM 5.3 Flash), Git worktree, and web fingerprinting.

阮一峰周刊第410期用通俗易懂的方式拆解了AI回答问题的三大机制：参数机制（把人类知识压缩进几千亿个权重里）、推理机制（靠逻辑推导出参数里没有的知识）、联网机制（通过Agent上网搜索前两者都搞不定的信息）。此外还聊了公开演讲的窍门、平板椅、虚拟化身演唱会等科技趣闻，以及盖茨对AI风险态度的转变、匿名模型Ox Alpha被证实是智谱GLM 5.3 Flash等文章。

  • LLMs answer questions through three mechanisms: parameters (compressed knowledge), reasoning (inferred knowledge), and web access (searched knowledge).
  • The parameter mechanism models all human knowledge as mathematical relationships between tokens, represented by billions of weights—essentially a 'compression-generation' process.
  • The anonymous model Ox Alpha was confirmed to be Zhipu's GLM 5.3 Flash, scoring 57 on Artificial Analysis—higher than DeepSeek V4 Pro (53)—despite having only 18B active parameters and being runnable locally.
💡 Why it matters: This issue offers developers a clear mental model for how LLMs work—parameters, reasoning, and web access—which is useful for understanding model capabilities and limitations, while the Ox Alpha/GLM 5.3 Flash news highlights the trend of smaller, locally runnable models achieving top-tier performance.
🇨🇳 这期内容帮开发者建立理解大模型运作的清晰框架（参数、推理、联网），有助于判断模型能力边界；而Ox Alpha实为GLM 5.3 Flash的消息则说明小参数、可本地运行的模型也能达到顶尖水平。

This week's issue of Ruan Yifeng's tech newsletter explains AI prompt caching: cached input tokens can cost as little as 1/50th of uncached input tokens because the model skips recomputing previously processed prefixes. Cache lifetimes vary by provider (Anthropic 5 min, DeepSeek/OpenAI 10-30 min, Google up to 1 hour), so AI agents often send periodic keep-alive requests—though the recommended interval is now every 4 minutes rather than 30 seconds. The issue also covers Bluetooth-reading traffic cameras, sonic fire extinguishing, windowless jets, and the longest line of sight on Earth.

阮一峰周刊第408期把「AI 缓存」讲透了：命中缓存的输入 Token 价格可能只有未命中的五十分之一，因为模型不用重复计算已经处理过的前缀。各家缓存有效期不同（Anthropic 5 分钟、DeepSeek/OpenAI 10~30 分钟、Google 最长 1 小时），所以 Agent 常靠定时「续命」请求保住缓存，但最新建议是每 4 分钟激活一次，而不是 30 秒。此外还有能读蓝牙的交通摄像头、声波灭火、无舷窗私人飞机等趣闻。

  • Cached input tokens can be up to 50x cheaper than uncached ones (e.g., DeepSeek V4 Flash: 0.02 vs 1 yuan), since cached prefixes require no recomputation.
  • Cache TTL differs by vendor: Anthropic ~5 min, DeepSeek ~10 min, OpenAI 10-30 min, Google up to 1 hour.
  • AI agents keep caches alive with periodic requests; the recommended interval has shifted from every 30 seconds to every 4 minutes to cut keep-alive costs.
💡 Why it matters: Developers building LLM agents should design prompt prefixes to be stable and reuse them within the provider's cache window, and tune keep-alive intervals—this can cut inference costs by an order of magnitude.
🇨🇳 做 LLM Agent 的开发者应尽量保持提示词前缀稳定，并在缓存有效期内复用，同时合理设置保活间隔——这能把推理成本降低一个数量级。
