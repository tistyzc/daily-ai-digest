  • AI is moving from screen-based chatbots into embodied forms—robots, smart glasses, earbuds, car cockpits—creating new interaction challenges beyond raw intelligence.
  • The key missing capability is 'social intelligence': knowing when to act, how to act, and when to stop, rather than just understanding user intent.
  • Thinking Machines Lab's Interaction Models process continuous audio, video, and text with ~200ms micro-turns, treating interaction as a core model architecture problem.
💡 Why it matters: Developers building AI products must now design for timing, restraint, and social context—not just capability—as interaction becomes a first-class engineering problem in embodied and ambient AI.
🇨🇳 做AI产品的开发者得开始把“分寸感”当正经需求来设计——交互时机、克制和社交语境，正在成为具身与 ambient AI 的一等工程问题。

极客公园 🔴 Factual
