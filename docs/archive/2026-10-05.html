This article examines why human-carrying robots remain slow and cautious despite advances in autonomous drones and mining trucks, arguing that the presence of a human rider fundamentally changes the robot's dynamics and safety requirements. It highlights that stability and safety, not speed or agility, are the core priorities for legged human-carrying robots, and explores practical applications such as rescue operations and mobility assistance for people with limited mobility.

无人机和无人矿卡早已实现自主作业，为什么载人机器人却依然走得慢吞吞？本文指出，一旦上面坐了人，机器人控制的就不再是单纯的机器，而是一套“机器人+人”的新动力学系统，稳定和安全才是底线。除了炫技，载人机器人真正的价值可能在于复杂地形救援和帮助行动不便的人拓展活动半径。

  • Practical applications include casualty evacuation in difficult terrain and mobility assistance for people with limited mobility.

当AI开始制造AI

钛媒体 🔴 Factual

The article examines the emerging trend of AI systems participating in the development of their own successors, known as recursive self-improvement (RSI). It highlights Google's AlphaEvolve, which improved Gemini's training efficiency by 23% on a key kernel, as a concrete example. However, the author cautions that faster iteration does not guarantee better outcomes, and that safety boundaries and evaluation criteria must remain under independent human oversight.

这篇文章探讨了AI开始参与制造下一代AI的趋势，即递归自我改进（RSI）。谷歌的AlphaEvolve已能优化Gemini训练，让关键计算核加速23%。但作者提醒，更快不等于更好，安全边界和评估标准不能只靠系统自己说了算，独立验证和人类判断仍然不可或缺。

  • AI is increasingly being used to improve AI itself, with Google's AlphaEvolve achieving a 23% speedup in a key Gemini computation kernel.
  • Recursive self-improvement (RSI) is distinct from artificial superintelligence (ASI); RSI is a process, while ASI is a capability goal.
  • A major challenge is defining and verifying 'improvement'—AI can optimize for a metric without meeting all human requirements, as seen in Klarna's experiment where fast code was not reproducible.
💡 Why it matters: Developers and AI practitioners should recognize that while AI-assisted RSI can accelerate progress, it requires robust independent verification and clear safety boundaries to avoid optimizing for the wrong metrics.
🇨🇳 开发者和AI从业者应认识到，AI辅助的递归自我改进虽能加速进步，但必须建立独立的验证机制和明确的安全边界，避免优化了错误指标。
