重控制、轻渲染，达瓦科技的 AI 视频工业路线

极客公园

Dawa Technology, a Chongqing-based startup founded in 2023 from a tech film studio, is pursuing a 'heavy control, light rendering' approach to AI video. Its Latent Stage base model separates scene understanding and control from pixel rendering, enabling precise, editable video generation with fewer retries. The company has raised ~500M RMB and reports ~$100M ARR, with products FilmOS for offline professional generation and Scenes AI, a real-time world model running on a single consumer GPU.

达瓦科技走了一条和主流视频大模型不同的路：不拼参数堆算力，而是把「控制」单独做成一层基座模型 Latent Stage，让导演能指哪改哪，少抽卡、少返工。公司靠真实片场攒下的 180TB 过程数据训练模型，已融资约 5 亿元，年 ARR 约 1 亿美元。旗下 FilmOS 做专业离线生成，Scenes AI 则能在单张消费级显卡上实时跑世界模型。

  • Dawa's Latent Stage control-layer model separates scene/actor/camera/lighting understanding from rendering, enabling local edits without regenerating entire shots.
  • The company claims its approach cuts video generation retries from 10-20 down to 3-4, reducing overall production cost by 50-60%.
  • Dawa has accumulated ~180TB of structured 'process data' from real film sets, including script, camera, lighting, and motion capture, used to train the control model.
💡 Why it matters: This signals a shift from brute-force scaling toward controllability and data efficiency in video generation, offering developers a blueprint for building production-grade AI video tools that reduce costly retries.
🇨🇳 这给视频生成指了条新路：与其盲目堆算力，不如把可控性和数据效率做上去，对想做影视级 AI 工具的开发者很有参考价值。

行业在喊数据不够，但 80% 的数据可能死于“只奖励走通”

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

The article argues that the bottleneck in embodied AI is not the non-standard nature of the physical world, but the training objective that only rewards task success, not physical understanding. Data retention rates are low (e.g., DROID's 20.7%), and models memorize scenes rather than learning transferable physics, leading to poor generalization and a project-based business model.

具身智能的瓶颈不在世界太非标，而在训练目标只奖励“把事做成”，从不要求模型理解物理规律。结果模型记住场景而非学会规律，数据复用率极低（如DROID保留率仅20.7%），泛化差导致只能做项目制，形成恶性循环。

  • Data retention is low: DROID dataset has only 20.7% retention, others 30-40%, best 60%.
  • Reward misalignment: models are trained to succeed, not to extract physical laws, so they memorize scenes.
  • Business impact: poor generalization leads to project-based deployments, e.g., 3 months training vs 1 week for traditional automation.
💡 Why it matters: Developers should rethink reward design in embodied AI: optimizing only for task success yields brittle models; incorporating physical-law extraction as an auxiliary objective could unlock true generalization and reduce data waste.
🇨🇳 开发者需重新思考具身智能的奖励设计：只优化任务成功会得到脆弱模型；将物理规律提炼作为辅助目标，才能实现真正泛化并减少数据浪费。

脑机接口临床试验，在全国抢病人

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

Chinese brain-computer interface (BCI) companies are increasingly shifting clinical trials away from top-tier hospitals in first-tier cities to provincial and prefecture-level hospitals, as leading centers face congestion in ethics approvals, PI time, and patient recruitment. Despite expanded trial capacity at major hospitals, extremely high patient exclusion rates mean enrollment remains slow, intensifying competition for limited clinical resources and investor attention.

国内脑机接口企业正把临床试验从北上广的顶级三甲医院，转向省会城市甚至周口、廊坊、绵阳、江阴等地市级医院，因为头部医院的伦理档期、PI时间和质控资源已经排不过来。但医院扩容速度远赶不上需求爆发，患者筛选排除率极高——天坛医院1600多人就诊仅约一成符合条件，河北医大二院2000多人报名只入选1人。临床资源争夺的背后，是企业抢滩资本市场第一股和融资窗口的焦虑。

  • BCI trials are expanding to provincial and prefecture-level hospitals in Shandong, Shanxi, Henan, Jiangsu and smaller cities like Zhoukou, Langfang, Mianyang and Jiangyin.
  • Top hospitals are overloaded with BCI project applications competing for limited ethics approval slots, PI time and quality control resources.
  • Patient recruitment is a major bottleneck: TianTan Hospital saw over 1,600 visitors by end-2025 with only ~10% eligible; Hebei Medical University Second Hospital enrolled just 1 of 2,000+ applicants.
💡 Why it matters: For BCI developers, clinical trial access and patient recruitment—not funding—are becoming the binding constraint, making hospital partnerships and data quality the decisive factors for regulatory approval and valuation.
🇨🇳 对脑机接口开发者来说，临床试验资源和患者入组能力正取代资金成为最紧的瓶颈，医院合作与数据质量将直接决定产品注册进度和公司估值。
