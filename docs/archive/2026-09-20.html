科技爱好者周刊（第 410 期）：你需要知道的 AI 三种机制

阮一峰周刊 🔴 Factual 🟢 Privacy

In the 410th issue of his weekly tech digest, Ruan Yifeng explains the three core mechanisms behind how large language models answer questions: the parameter mechanism (knowledge compressed into billions of weights via training), the reasoning mechanism (deriving knowledge not stored in parameters through logic), and the web-access mechanism (using agents to search online for information the model neither stores nor can infer). The issue also covers public speaking tips, tech news like a flat-pack chair and virtual avatar concerts, and articles on AI risks, the anonymous Ox Alpha model (revealed to be Zhipu's GLM 5.3 Flash), and a Google Workspace domain bug.

阮一峰周刊第410期用通俗语言讲清了AI回答问题的三大机制：参数机制把人类知识压缩进数千亿权重里，推理机制靠逻辑推导出参数里没存的知识，联网机制则让Agent上网搜索前两者都搞不定的信息。本期还聊了公开演讲的窍门、能拆成平板的椅子、虚拟化身演唱会，以及比尔·盖茨对AI态度的转变、匿名模型Ox Alpha被证实是智谱GLM 5.3 Flash等科技动态。

  • LLMs work through three mechanisms: parameters (knowledge compressed into weights during training), reasoning (deriving knowledge not stored in parameters), and web access (agents searching online for the rest).
  • Zhipu's GLM 5.3 Flash was revealed as the anonymous model Ox Alpha, scoring 57 on Artificial Analysis — beating DeepSeek V4 Pro's 53 despite having only 18B active parameters vs 49B.
  • Bill Gates published an article arguing AI development should slow down, contradicting his 2023 piece claiming AI risks were controllable.
💡 Why it matters: The three-mechanism framework gives developers a clean mental model for when to rely on model parameters, when to prompt for reasoning, and when to wire up retrieval or agent-based web search — a practical guide for designing LLM applications.
🇨🇳 这套"三机制"框架帮开发者理清思路：什么时候靠模型参数、什么时候靠推理、什么时候该接检索或联网Agent，对设计LLM应用很实用。

科技爱好者周刊（第 408 期）：你需要知道的 AI 缓存知识

阮一峰周刊 🟢 Privacy

This week's digest explains AI prompt caching: cached input tokens cost up to 50x less than uncached ones because the model reuses prior computations instead of recalculating them. Cache lifetimes vary by provider (Anthropic 5 min, DeepSeek/OpenAI 10-30 min, Google up to 1 hour), and agents often send periodic keep-alive requests to avoid expensive cache misses. The issue also covers Bluetooth-reading traffic cameras, sonic fire extinguishing, windowless jets, and the longest line of sight on Earth.

本期周刊把「AI 缓存」讲透了：命中缓存的输入 Token 价格可能只有未命中的五十分之一，因为模型直接复用之前的计算结果，省下了算力。各家缓存有效期差别不小——Anthropic 只有 5 分钟，DeepSeek、OpenAI 约 10 到 30 分钟，Google 最长 1 小时，所以不少 Agent 会定时发请求「续命」缓存。此外还聊了能识别蓝牙的交通摄像头、用声波灭火、没有舷窗的飞机，以及地球上最长的视线。

  • Cached input tokens can be up to 50x cheaper than uncached input tokens, since cached prefixes require almost no compute.
  • Cache lifetimes differ by provider: Anthropic ~5 minutes, DeepSeek ~10 minutes, OpenAI 10-30 minutes, Google up to 1 hour.
  • AI agents often send keep-alive requests every 30 seconds to keep caches warm; a 4-minute interval is now recommended.
💡 Why it matters: Developers building LLM agents should design prompt prefixes to be cache-friendly and tune keep-alive intervals, since cache misses can inflate costs by orders of magnitude.
🇨🇳 做 LLM Agent 的开发者要注意把提示词前缀设计得利于缓存命中，并合理设置保活间隔，否则一次缓存未命中就可能让成本翻几十倍。
