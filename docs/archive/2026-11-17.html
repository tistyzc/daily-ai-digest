男性站着尿比坐着尿更健康？随机试验发现差异不显著

虎嗅 (AI原生100等)

A randomized crossover trial by researchers at the Colombian Urology Research Group found no significant difference in maximum urine flow rate or total voided volume between standing and sitting urination in 52 healthy young men. The only statistically significant difference was the time to reach maximum flow, which was 3.32 seconds longer when standing, but this is likely not clinically meaningful. The study also highlights that 'no significant difference' does not prove equivalence, as the equivalence test failed.

哥伦比亚泌尿学研究小组的一项随机交叉试验发现，对健康年轻男性来说，站着尿和坐着尿的最大尿流率和总排尿量没有显著差别。唯一有统计学差异的是达到最大尿流率的时间，站着排尿平均多花3.32秒，但研究者认为这没有临床意义。研究还提醒，“没有显著差异”不等于“证明两者一样”，等效性检验并未通过。

  • Randomized crossover trial with 52 healthy men aged 18-39 found no significant difference in maximum flow rate (P=0.164) or total volume (P=0.318) between standing and sitting.
  • Time to maximum flow rate was 3.32 seconds longer when standing (P=0.011), but deemed not clinically meaningful.
  • Equivalence test failed because the 95% CI lower bound (-3.55 ml/s) exceeded the predefined ±2.5 ml/s margin, so equivalence cannot be claimed.
💡 Why it matters: For developers and AI practitioners, this study is a reminder that 'no significant difference' is not proof of equivalence—a statistical nuance that matters when interpreting A/B tests and model comparisons.
🇨🇳 对开发者和AI从业者来说，这项研究提醒我们“无显著差异”不等于“证明等效”——在解读A/B测试和模型对比时，这个统计学细节至关重要。
