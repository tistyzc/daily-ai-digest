  • Displacement is layered: augmentation first, then bypass—early adopters who feed systems their feedback can be replaced by those same systems.
💡 Why it matters: For developers and AI practitioners, the article is a reminder that building on top of AI tools is not a moat—proprietary data, domain judgment, and the willingness to abandon obsolete skills are what determine who survives the transition.
