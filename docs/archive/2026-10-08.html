给神经装上“光开关”，他们拿下诺奖：让失明者重新看见，还差几步？

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel for foundational work on light-controlled ion channels and optogenetics. Their technique, which uses light to control genetically modified cells, has already enabled partial vision restoration in a patient and has led to an optogenetic therapy (MCO-010) now under regulatory review for retinitis pigmentosa. However, significant challenges remain in translating this into a broadly available treatment.

2026年诺贝尔生理学或医学奖授予了卡尔·戴瑟罗斯、彼得·黑格曼和格奥尔格·纳格尔，表彰他们在光控离子通道和光遗传学领域的奠基性工作。这项技术通过给细胞装上“光开关”来操控神经活动，已帮助一名视网膜色素变性患者恢复部分视觉，相关疗法MCO-010也已进入上市审评阶段。但从个案到普及治疗，仍有很长的路要走。

  • The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded for the discovery of light-controlled ion channels and optogenetics.
  • Optogenetics uses gene therapy to make specific cells light-sensitive, then uses light to control their activity, revolutionizing brain research.
  • A 2021 study showed a patient with retinitis pigmentosa could perform a task with a special headset, demonstrating partial vision restoration.
💡 Why it matters: This breakthrough offers a new paradigm for treating genetic diseases by bypassing specific mutations, but developers must navigate complex clinical and regulatory hurdles to prove broad efficacy and safety.
🇨🇳 这一突破为治疗遗传性疾病提供了绕过特定突变的新思路，但开发者仍需克服复杂的临床和监管障碍，以证明其广泛疗效和安全性。

把5160只“眼睛”埋进南极：哈尔岑追踪“幽灵粒子”，获物理学诺奖

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics has been awarded solely to Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison for his pivotal role in creating the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy astrophysical neutrinos. IceCube, buried in a cubic kilometer of Antarctic ice with 5,160 optical sensors, overcame early setbacks like bubble scattering and engineering challenges to detect 28 high-energy events in 2013, later confirmed at 5.7 sigma, and in 2017 helped trace a neutrino to a blazar, opening a new era of multi-messenger astronomy.

2026年诺贝尔物理学奖独授予弗朗西斯·哈尔岑，表彰他主导建造冰立方中微子天文台并发现天体高能中微子。这个埋在南极一立方公里冰层下、拥有5160只“眼睛”的巨型探测器，克服了冰中气泡干扰和极地施工难题，于2013年捕捉到28个关键事件，2017年更追踪到一颗来自耀变体的中微子，开启了多信使天文学的新窗口。

  • Francis Halzen is the sole recipient of the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for his decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy astrophysical neutrinos.
  • IceCube consists of 86 strings with 5,160 optical sensors deployed between 1,450 and 2,450 meters deep in Antarctic ice, covering about one cubic kilometer.
  • Early prototype encountered bubble scattering at 800–1000 m depth, forcing the team to deploy deeper where ice is clearer.
💡 Why it matters: This validates neutrino astronomy as a powerful new window to study the most energetic cosmic processes, and the engineering and data-analysis techniques developed for IceCube will inspire future large-scale detectors and multi-messenger astronomy.
🇨🇳 这证明了中微子天文学是研究宇宙极端过程的有力新窗口，冰立方所积累的工程与数据分析经验将推动未来更大规模探测器和多信使天文学的发展。
