昨晚，硅谷经历了AI诸神之战。

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🟡 Bias

Last night in Silicon Valley, Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash, Meta unveiled Muse Spark 1.3, and startup Mostik was featured by WIRED. While both models topped the DeepSWE benchmark, the real shift is in AI economics: the focus is moving from cost per million tokens to the cost of completing real-world agentic tasks. 昨晚硅谷三件大事：Google发布Gemini 3.8 Flash，Meta发布Muse Spark 1.3，初创公司Mostik登上WIRED。表面是刷榜，但真正的变化在AI经济学——成本计量正从“每百万token多少钱”转向“完成一个真实任务多少钱”。Gemini 3.8 Flash在DeepSWE上约74%准确率，单任务成本仅2.36美元，碾压对手；Meta的Muse Spark 1.3则通过减少工具调用和token消耗来优化Agent效率。Mostik更激进：AI之间对话何必用人类语言？这可能是更深层的革命。

  • Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash achieves ~74% on DeepSWE v1.1 at an average cost of $2.36 per task, significantly cheaper than Claude Opus 5 ($11.84) and GPT-5.6 Sol ($6.46).
  • Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 scores 75.4% on DeepSWE, a ~20-point jump from 1.2, while reducing tool calls by ~20% and token consumption by ~25%.
  • Startup Mostik proposes eliminating human-language tokens in AI-to-AI communication, potentially redefining agent efficiency.
💡 Why it matters: This marks a pivotal shift for developers: optimizing for task-level cost and agent reliability becomes more critical than raw model capability, and new models like Gemini 3.8 Flash make frontier-level coding agents affordable for broader adoption.
🇨🇳 对开发者而言，这意味着优化重点从模型能力转向任务级成本和Agent可靠性；Gemini 3.8 Flash等模型让前沿级编码Agent变得可负担，将加速AI Agent的规模化落地。
