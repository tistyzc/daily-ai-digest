阮一峰周刊 🔴 Factual 🟢 Privacy

In the 410th issue of his weekly tech digest, Ruan Yifeng explains the three core mechanisms behind how large language models answer questions: the parameter mechanism (knowledge compressed into billions of weights via training), the reasoning mechanism (deriving knowledge not stored in parameters through logical inference), and the web-search mechanism (retrieving information that neither parameters nor reasoning can provide). The issue also covers public speaking tips, tech news like a flat-pack chair and virtual avatar concerts, and articles on Git worktree, WebAudio fingerprinting, and a Google Workspace domain bug.

阮一峰周刊第410期用大白话讲清了AI回答问题的三大机制：参数机制把人类知识压缩进几千亿个权重里，推理机制靠逻辑推导出参数里没存的知识，联网机制则负责搜索前两者都搞不定的信息。本期还聊了公开演讲的窍门、平板椅和虚拟化身演唱会等科技动态，以及Git worktree、WebAudio指纹识别、谷歌域名Bug等实用文章。

  • LLMs answer questions through three mechanisms: parameters (compressed knowledge), reasoning (inferred knowledge), and web search (retrieved knowledge).

  • The reasoning mechanism derives knowledge not stored in parameters, such as calculating population growth rate from birth and death rates.
💡 Why it matters: This article provides developers and AI practitioners with a clear, non-technical mental model of how LLMs work, helping them better understand model capabilities and limitations when building AI applications.
🇨🇳 这篇文章用通俗易懂的方式帮开发者和AI从业者建立大模型运作的心智模型，有助于在开发AI应用时更好地理解模型的能力边界。
