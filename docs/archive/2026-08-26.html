硅谷押注的下一个 Harness，是整个桌面操作系统

极客公园

Omarchy, a Linux distribution by DHH (creator of Ruby on Rails), released version 4.0 on August 14, positioning itself as an 'AI-native OS' where AI agents can directly read and modify the system. The project secured $8 million in initial funding from eight tech luminaries including Michael Dell and Jack Dorsey, and has seen rapid community adoption with over 1,000 plugins within ten days. Unlike Apple and Microsoft's approach of keeping AI as a button, Omarchy makes the entire system text-based and agent-accessible, effectively turning the OS itself into a harness.

Omarchy 是一个由 Ruby on Rails 作者 DHH 打造的 Linux 发行版，8 月 14 日发布 4.0 版本，主打「AI 原生操作系统」——AI 代理能直接读写系统配置、操作电脑，而不是像苹果微软那样只给个按钮。8 月 21 日，Michael Dell、Jack Dorsey 等八位硅谷大佬每人出资 100 万美元成立 Omacom 基金会，首期 800 万美元。发布十天社区就写了 1074 个插件，生态爆发力惊人。这波操作把「一切皆文件」的 Unix 老传统翻出来，让 AI 真正接管桌面，而不是替你做决定。

  • Omarchy 4.0 rewrites the entire desktop environment into a single process (Quickshell), with all configs as Lua scripts and plugins as Git repos, making every part of the system readable and modifiable by AI agents.
  • The system ships with nine default AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, etc.) that can be authorized once and then operate the system via natural language commands.
  • The $8 million Omacom Foundation funding supports infrastructure, trademark, and open-source dependencies, with the first grant going to Hyprland's author.

🇨🇳 这意味着操作系统开始把 AI 代理当作一等公民来设计，可能打破 Windows/macOS 的垄断，给开发者一个让 AI 自主完成系统级任务的新平台。
