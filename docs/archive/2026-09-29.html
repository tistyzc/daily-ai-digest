陈华锋、张虎来：经济放缓时，人会更谨慎吗？1000万份车险保单里的风险偏好

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

Researchers Chen Huafeng and Zhang Hulai from Fudan University analyzed approximately 10 million Chinese auto insurance policies (2011–2017, covering ~2.95 million policyholders) to estimate individual risk aversion from deductible choices. They found that when provincial GDP growth slows by one standard deviation (~1.8 percentage points), estimated relative risk aversion rises by about 36.6%, suggesting risk preferences are countercyclical rather than fixed personality traits. Cross-validation showed regions with higher inferred risk aversion had lower lottery sales and stock trading volumes but higher insurance and bond sales.

复旦大学的陈华锋和张虎来利用约1000万份中国车险保单（2011–2017年，覆盖约295万投保人），从免赔额选择中估算个人风险厌恶程度。研究发现，省级GDP增速每下降一个标准差（约1.8个百分点），估算的相对风险厌恶指标上升约36.6%，说明风险偏好会随经济周期反向波动，而非一成不变的性格特质。交叉验证还发现，风险厌恶较高的地区彩票和股票交易更少，保险和债券销售更多。

  • Analyzed ~10 million auto insurance policies from 2011–2017 covering ~2.95 million unique policyholders in China, using deductible choices to infer risk aversion.
  • A one standard deviation decline in provincial real quarterly GDP growth (~1.8 pp) is associated with a ~36.6% increase in estimated relative risk aversion.
  • Risk aversion appears countercyclical: the same individual becomes more risk-averse when economic growth slows, challenging the view that risk preferences are stable traits.
💡 Why it matters: This research provides large-scale empirical evidence that risk preferences are dynamic and countercyclical, which has direct implications for how financial models price risk, how robo-advisors and fintech platforms should adapt to macroeconomic conditions, and how AI systems modeling human decision-making should incorporate time-varying risk attitudes rather than assuming fixed utility functions.
🇨🇳 这项研究用大规模实证数据表明风险偏好是动态且逆周期的，对金融风险定价模型、智能投顾和金融科技平台如何随宏观经济调整策略，以及AI系统建模人类决策时是否应引入时变风险态度，都有直接启示。
