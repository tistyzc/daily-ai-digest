给AI爱因斯坦的知识量，它能想出相对论吗？

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

Scientists are testing whether large language models trained only on historical data up to a certain cutoff (e.g., 1911) can rediscover major scientific breakthroughs like Einstein's general relativity, a concept proposed by DeepMind's Demis Hassabis as a test for AGI. Early attempts, including independent researcher Michael Hla's 'Machina Mirabilis' model, show that current LLMs mostly fail such tests, often producing plausible-sounding but physically incoherent answers and lacking a robust internal world model. 科学家们正在测试一个有趣的问题：如果只给AI喂到1911年为止的知识，它能不能自己重新发现爱因斯坦的广义相对论？DeepMind的Hassabis把这当作检验AGI的“爱因斯坦测试”。不过初步尝试结果不太乐观——独立研究员训练的复古模型大多只会“鹦鹉学舌”，说些乍看有理但经不起推敲的话，缺乏真正的物理世界内部表征。研究者认为，当前大模型擅长归纳和预测，却搞不定那种需要“灵光一现”的溯因推理。

  • DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis proposed the 'Einstein test': train an LLM on all knowledge before 1911 and see if it can rediscover general relativity, as a benchmark for AGI.
  • Independent researcher Michael Hla built 'Machina Mirabilis' using pre-1900 data; the model occasionally showed 'flashes of intuition' but mostly failed, lacking a coherent internal world model.
  • A paper titled 'LLMs can't jump' argues that current models excel at inductive reasoning but cannot perform the abductive leaps needed for paradigm shifts.
💡 Why it matters: This research highlights a fundamental limitation of current LLMs—their inability to perform creative abductive reasoning—which is crucial for developers aiming to build AGI or AI systems capable of genuine scientific discovery.
🇨🇳 这项研究揭示了当前大模型的一个根本局限——缺乏创造性溯因推理能力，这对致力于构建AGI或让AI真正参与科学发现的开发者来说，是一个关键警示。

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

The article uses a train passenger's experience of being unable to move between carriages of an 8+8 coupled train to illustrate how 'bugs' arise in complex systems: each component works as designed, but the relationships between them are overlooked. It draws on historical examples such as the Han dynasty iron tool debate, London's 1992 ambulance dispatch failure, the 1858 'Great Stink', Braess's paradox, and the 19th-century railway gauge mismatch to argue that system simplification, unmodeled real-world dynamics, and local optimization without end-to-end coordination produce failures that no single component's metrics can catch.

一张分段购买的有效车票，却因为8+8重联列车两组车厢之间隔着驾驶室而无法换座——票卖了、座位在、车也到了，乘客却享受不到已付费的服务。文章用这个令人窒息的Bug串起盐铁会议上的农具、1992年伦敦救护车调度系统失灵、1858年伦敦大恶臭、布雷斯悖论和19世纪英国轨距之争，指出复杂系统的故障往往不是某个环节没做好，而是系统只记录了对象属性、却漏掉了对象之间的关系，以及局部效率叠加后整体反而崩掉。

  • The train case shows a system can validate every individual record (ticket, seat, train arrival) while missing the relational constraint of whether a passenger can physically move between carriages.
  • Scott's concept of 'legibility' explains why large-scale management simplifies reality into uniform categories, discarding differences that still matter on the ground.
  • London's 1992 ambulance dispatch system ran as designed but failed because it over-relied on timely, accurate status updates that busy field staff could not always provide.
💡 Why it matters: For developers and AI practitioners, it is a reminder that data models must capture relationships and end-to-end task feasibility, not just entity attributes, and that optimizing component metrics without system-level validation can ship 'correct' systems that fail users.
🇨🇳 对开发者和AI从业者来说，这提醒我们：数据模型要建模对象之间的关系和任务端到端的可行性，而不只是字段属性；只优化局部指标、不做系统级验证，就可能交付一个“处处正确”却让用户用不了的系统。
