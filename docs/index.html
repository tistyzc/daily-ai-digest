造得出一万台机器人，却喂不饱一个大脑

钛媒体

Humanoid robots are entering mass production, but their 'brains' face a severe data famine: unlike LLMs that train on free internet text, robots require costly, slow, and fragile real-world physical interaction data. The industry is stuck in a vicious cycle where robots need real-world data to become smart, but need to be smart to safely enter real-world scenarios, and current simulation and teleoperation methods cannot scale.

人形机器人硬件已进入量产时代，但“大脑”却遭遇数据饥荒：大模型靠互联网免费文本训练，机器人却需要昂贵、缓慢且易损耗的真实物理交互数据。行业陷入死循环——机器人需要真实数据才能变聪明，但需要足够聪明才能安全进入真实场景，而仿真和遥操作目前都无法规模化解决。

  • Humanoid robot hardware is mass-producing (e.g., Agibot's 15,000th unit), but training data for physical interaction is scarce and expensive.
  • LLMs use free internet text (e.g., Common Crawl with 250B tokens), while robot data requires real-world operations, costing time and money per sample.
  • Simulation (sim2real) fails due to inaccurate contact modeling, and teleoperation is costly and not scalable.
💡 Why it matters: This data bottleneck is the critical constraint for embodied AI; developers must innovate on data collection methods (e.g., synthetic data, learning from human videos, or shared datasets) to break the cycle and unlock general-purpose robot capabilities.
🇨🇳 数据瓶颈是具身智能发展的关键制约，开发者需在数据采集方法上创新（如合成数据、人类视频学习或共享数据集），才能打破循环，解锁通用机器人能力。
