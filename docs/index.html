社会共同体的大脑同频机制：看不见的群体烙印

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

A 2026 PNAS study found that members of tight-knit religious communities show far stronger brain-to-brain synchronization than secular individuals when watching the same videos, even neutral content like weather forecasts. This synchronization spans nearly all 17 functional brain networks and persists despite similar behavioral diversity, suggesting long-term social homogeneity leaves a durable neural imprint, not just momentary shared interpretation.

2026年PNAS上一项研究挺有意思：让宗教人士和世俗人士看同样的视频，结果宗教群体的大脑活动同步程度碾压世俗组，连看天气预报这种无聊内容都同步得整整齐齐。更绝的是，问卷和文字描述上两组人一样各抒己见，但大脑却像被同一个节拍器校准过——说明长期的社会化实践不仅影响你信什么，还悄悄重塑了你大脑感知世界的方式。

  • Religious participants showed significantly higher intersubject correlation (ISC) than secular participants across 50 of 51 cross-network comparisons, including low-level sensory areas, not just high-order networks.
  • Despite strong neural synchronization, behavioral measures (emotion, interest, attitude) showed no significant group differences in within-group variance, and secular participants even had slightly higher semantic similarity in text descriptions.
  • An SVM classifier using only neural synchronization patterns could decode religious vs. secular identity with 64.3%–92.0% accuracy, with precuneus and ventral occipital cortex as key regions.
💡 Why it matters: This suggests neural synchronization could serve as a biomarker for social group membership, with implications for designing more personalized brain-computer interfaces and understanding how cultural environments shape information processing in AI models trained on group-specific data.
🇨🇳 这意味着神经同步可能成为社会群体身份的生物学标记，对设计个性化脑机接口、以及理解文化环境如何塑造AI模型的信息处理方式都有启发。
