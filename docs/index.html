The author cites the 2026 JigShape study showing a 'scale cliff' where frontier vision models collapse beyond 16-64 pieces, and notes that classical computer vision pipelines with precise contour extraction and global optimization can actually solve such puzzles.

一位作者花12天拼完1000片纯白地狱拼图，随后让大模型看照片重拼，结果五小时就崩了：模型只会描述边缘'凸得比较陡'，给不出'凸点在37.2%处'这种可测量的数据，推30片时还给出A接B、B接C、C又接A的闭环。作者引用JigShape研究指出，前沿视觉模型在16片以上就开始'规模悬崖'式崩塌；反倒是40年前的经典机器视觉方案——精确轮廓提取加全局优化——真能拼。

  • The model could describe edges qualitatively but could not produce precise measurements, and its descriptions of the same piece varied between runs.
  • It failed to maintain a globally consistent puzzle state, producing a circular A-B-C-A pairing when asked to chain 30 pieces.
