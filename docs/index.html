重控制、轻渲染，达瓦科技的 AI 视频工业路线

极客公园 🔴 Factual 🟡 Bias

Dawa Technology, a Chongqing-based startup founded in 2023 from a film studio operator, has raised about 500 million RMB to pursue a 'control-heavy, rendering-light' approach to AI video. Its Latent Stage base model separates scene understanding and control from pixel rendering, powering FilmOS for professional offline generation and Scenes AI, a real-time world model that runs on a single consumer GPU. The company claims its 180TB of structured on-set process data cuts 'slot-machine' retries by 50-60% and supports roughly $100M ARR.

达瓦科技走了一条和主流视频大模型不一样的路：不拼参数堆算力，而是把「控制」单独做成一层基座模型 Latent Stage，让导演像指挥片场一样指挥 AI，渲染层只负责出画面。靠着片场攒下的 180TB 过程数据，他们号称能把「抽卡」次数砍掉一半以上，还能在单张消费级显卡上实时跑世界模型。

  • Dawa splits AI video into a control layer (Latent Stage) and a rendering layer, aiming for editable, physically consistent output instead of repeated re-rolls.
  • Products include FilmOS for offline professional generation and Scenes AI, a real-time world model running on one consumer-grade GPU.
  • The company has raised about 500M RMB in Pre-A and A rounds and reports roughly $100M ARR.
💡 Why it matters: This signals a shift from scaling generic video models toward domain-specific control layers, suggesting developers should think about structured process data and controllability as key differentiators for industrial AI video.
🇨🇳 这提醒开发者：视频生成的下一战可能不在堆参数，而在控制层和高质量过程数据，谁能把「不可控」变成「指哪打哪」，谁就能啃下专业影视这块硬骨头。
