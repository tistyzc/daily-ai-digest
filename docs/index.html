生命密码必须 ATCG4个字母？新研究发现8个字母，细胞机器也照单全收

虎嗅 (AI原生100等) 🔴 Factual

All known life uses four DNA bases (A, T, C, G), but researchers have long asked whether this is a physical necessity or an evolutionary accident. In 2019, Steven Benner's team built Hachimoji DNA, an eight-letter genetic system combining natural A-T and G-C pairs with unnatural P-Z and B-S pairs, and showed it retains normal B-form structure and predictable thermodynamics. Recent work further confirms that this eight-letter DNA can be transcribed into RNA by engineered RNA polymerase, suggesting the genetic alphabet is not fixed.

地球生命都用ATCG四个字母写DNA，但科学家一直想知道：这是物理规律逼出来的，还是演化碰巧选的？2019年，Benner团队造出了八字母的Hachimoji DNA，把天然碱基对和人工碱基对混在一起，结构居然和天然DNA一样稳定。最近的研究更进一步，证明这套八字母DNA还能被RNA聚合酶转录成RNA——生命的字母表，看来真不是固定不变的。

  • Hachimoji DNA uses eight nucleotides: natural A-T and G-C plus unnatural P-Z and B-S pairs.
  • The eight-letter system maintains normal B-form double helix geometry and predictable thermal stability.
  • Recent research shows Hachimoji DNA can be transcribed into RNA by engineered RNA polymerase.
💡 Why it matters: This expands the theoretical basis for synthetic biology and genetic engineering, opening possibilities for storing more information in DNA and creating semi-synthetic organisms with expanded genetic codes.
🇨🇳 这为合成生物学和基因工程拓展了理论基础，未来或许能在DNA里存更多信息，甚至造出带扩展遗传密码的半合成生物。

欧洲错失的机遇

虎嗅 (AI原生100等)

MIT Sloan chief scientist Andrew McAfee argues in Foreign Affairs that Europe's labor productivity has fallen from near parity with the US to 20% below it, largely because Europe missed the digital revolution and lacks global-scale tech firms—only France's Mistral competes in large language models. He attributes the stagnation to aging incumbents (the EU's top 50 firms average over 150 years old) and burdensome tech regulation like the AI Act, while the US regenerates itself through new startups such as Tesla and SpaceX.

麻省理工斯隆管理学院的Andrew McAfee在《外交事务》上发文直言：欧洲劳动生产率已从接近美国的95%跌到落后20%，罪魁祸首是错过了数字革命——云计算、互联网、芯片设计都没长出全球巨头，大模型赛道也只剩法国的Mistral一根独苗。他还吐槽欧盟的《人工智能法案》合规成本高得吓人，创业者和投资人干脆用脚投票跑路；反观美国，老巨头倒了还有特斯拉、SpaceX顶上，欧洲却只能守着大众、奔驰这些百年老店干着急。

  • EU labor productivity fell from ~95% of US level at the turn of the century to 20% below it today.
  • Europe lacks global-scale companies in cloud, internet, mobile devices and chip design; only Mistral competes in LLMs.
  • The EU's top 50 most valuable firms average over 150 years old, making them slow to change.
💡 Why it matters: For AI practitioners, this is a cautionary tale: heavy-handed regulation can push innovation and capital to friendlier jurisdictions, and Europe's weakness in industrial software plus AI is a gap that startups and investors elsewhere—especially in China—may exploit.
🇨🇳 对AI从业者来说，这是一记警钟：监管过重会把创新和资本赶到别处；欧洲工业软件强、AI弱，这个错位正是其他地区创业者和投资人可以切入的机会。
