擎天柱的手，到底有多难造？

钛媒体 🔴 Factual

Tesla's Optimus engineering lead recently highlighted dexterous hands as one of the most challenging hardware components, noting the conflicting demands of softness for handling fragile objects and durability for repeated use. The article breaks down the mechanical difficulties of tasks like picking up and handing over a pair of glasses, covering configuration, force transmission, grasp stability, tactile sensing, torque control, and wear resistance. It concludes that paper specifications do not equal practical capability, and sustained reliable operation in industrial settings remains a major hurdle for dexterous hands.

特斯拉Optimus工程负责人最近坦言，灵巧手是最具挑战的硬件之一——手指既要柔软到能拿易碎品，又要经得起反复磨损。文章以“捡眼镜并递给人”为例，拆解了构型、传力、抓握稳定、触觉感知、力矩控制和耐久性等多环节的相互制约。结论很直白：纸面参数不等于实用能力，工业场景下持续可靠作业仍是灵巧手必须翻越的门槛。

  • Tesla's Optimus team identifies dexterous hands as a top hardware challenge due to the trade-off between softness for fragile objects and durability for repeated use.
  • Picking up and handing over glasses exposes multiple coupled problems: finger configuration, approach direction, grasp point switching, friction, torque balance, and force transmission.
  • More joints and active degrees of freedom enable finer control but increase complexity in actuation, sensing, and coordination.
💡 Why it matters: Developers working on robotics and embodied AI should recognize that dexterous manipulation is a systems-level challenge spanning mechanics, actuation, sensing, and control—not just a matter of adding more degrees of freedom or improving individual components.
🇨🇳 对机器人和具身智能开发者来说，灵巧操作是涉及机械、驱动、传感和控制的系统级难题，不是单纯堆自由度或优化单个部件就能解决的。
