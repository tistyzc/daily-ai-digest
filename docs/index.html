把一立方公里的南极冰变成望远镜，他38年后拿下诺奖

钛媒体 🔴 Factual

Francis Halzen won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for the IceCube neutrino telescope, built from a cubic kilometer of Antarctic ice, after a 38-year effort. The award highlights a global race in neutrino astronomy, with China's newly deployed TRIDENT (Hailing) array in the South China Sea and the JUNO experiment in Jiangmen making rapid progress. China has also broken the foreign monopoly on large photomultiplier tubes, a key detector component.

等了几十年，中微子天文学终于拿下诺奖——冰立方把南极一立方公里的冰变成了望远镜。而中国这边也没闲着：南海3500米深处的“海铃”刚放下第一根串列，江门地下700米的JUNO已经跑出全球最高精度。更解气的是，曾被日本垄断的“探测器视网膜”光电倍增管，如今国产货不仅装进了自家装置，还逼得国外厂商降价。

  • Francis Halzen received the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for conceiving and leading the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, which uses a cubic kilometer of Antarctic ice as a detector.
  • China's TRIDENT (Hailing) project deployed its first telescope string prototype in the South China Sea at a depth of 3,500 meters, aiming to build the world's first near-equatorial deep-sea neutrino telescope by around 2030.
  • The JUNO experiment in Jiangmen, China, published its first physics results in Nature, achieving the world's highest precision for two key neutrino oscillation parameters within 59 days of data taking.
💡 Why it matters: This matters because it signals China's rise as a major player in neutrino astronomy and detector technology, potentially accelerating discoveries about the universe's highest-energy sources and reducing reliance on foreign suppliers for critical scientific components.
🇨🇳 这意味着中国正在成为中微子天文学和探测器技术的重要力量，有望加速对宇宙最高能天体源的探索，并减少对国外关键科学部件供应商的依赖。

This article analyzes the hard-discount business model of Aldi, based on a rare first-person account by founder Karl Albrecht.   • Aldi's founding principle was extreme SKU reduction, not low price: by 1953 each store carried only about 250-280 products, avoiding 'parallel goods' within the same category.

  • Low prices are a necessary compensation for reduced choice; Aldi cuts retail prices immediately when procurement prices fall, sacrificing short-term profit to build long-term price trust.
